# Global Layoffs Analysis 2020-2025

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing global layoffs trends across industries, companies, and regions from 2020 to 2025.

![Layoffs Dashboard](dashboard_layoffs.png)

## Project Overview
This project visualizes layoff data to identify patterns in industry impact, company size, funding stage, and geography. The goal is to understand how economic shifts affected workforce reduction in the tech and consumer sectors.

**Total Layoffs**: 582K+
**Time Period**: 2020 - 2025
**Data Source**: (https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ulrikeherold/tech-layoffs-2020-2024?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## Key Insights

**1. Layoffs peaked in 2023 at ~180K, reflecting a post-COVID hiring correction**
After aggressive hiring in 2021–2022, tech and consumer companies cut headcount sharply as growth slowed and interest rates rose.

**2. Consumer, Retail, and Hardware account for over 45% of total layoffs**
Of 582K layoffs, these three sectors were hit hardest as inflation and reduced consumer spending forced companies to cut costs quickly.

**3. Public companies drove the majority of job cuts**
Post-IPO firms account for 70%+ of layoffs. Investor pressure for profitability pushed public companies to reduce headcount faster than private or VC-backed firms.

**4. The U.S. is the largest contributor, concentrated in Big Tech**
Companies like Intel, Amazon, Microsoft, and Meta alone account for 100K+ layoffs.


## Dashboard Features
- **Layoffs by Industry**: Bar chart showing top affected sectors
- **Layoffs Trend by Year**: Line chart tracking annual trends
- **Top Companies**: Companies with highest layoff counts
- **Layoffs by Stage**: Breakdown by company funding stage
- **Layoffs by Country**: Geographic distribution of job cuts
- **KPI Cards**: Total layoffs, affected companies, and countries
